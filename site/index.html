Adapted from PRAWNS. Key generation is an honest-majority MPC among the n operators, with no dealer, that builds the XMSS part alone, never the few-time signatures: the kept subtree, and the pseudorandom surrogate nodes that replace the siblings above it.the top layer. Preprocessing, before a message: the operators pick 16 instances at random in the kept subtree and MPC-generate their forest keys, WOTS+C signatures and authentication pathsthe trees of the lower layers, the WOTS+C signatures that link them up to the top layer, and the authentication paths; a signature consumes one prepared instance, refilled with a fresh random one. Online: they grind the randomizer in the clear onto one of the 16, and threshold-open only the forest chain values the message selects. Secure against t − 1 malicious signers (with abort).
