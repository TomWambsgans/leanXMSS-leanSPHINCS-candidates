Adapted from PRAWNS: a one-time ceremony at keygen precomputes every hash of a secret, run either as an MPC among the operators (DKG) or by a trusted dealer; signing then needs no MPC (432 bytes per signer, 3 rounds), and is secure against t − 1 malicious signers (with abort), assuming an honest majority at keygen (DKG) or a trusted dealer.
