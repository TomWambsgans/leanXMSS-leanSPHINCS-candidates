A stateful hash-based signature for Ethereum attestations: $2^{32}$ signatures, small and fast to verify,
and thresholdizable with a DKG. Same hash as leanSPHINCS: $\hash(P,\mathit{tw},M)$ is BLAKE2s truncated to
16 bytes, costs are in compressions, and security is NIST level 1.

\section{The scheme}

\begin{itemize}
  \item synchronized: the leaf is the slot, so the only state is the slashing protection validators
        already keep (never sign two messages in one slot)
  \item two layers of XMSS of height 16: each leaf of the top tree signs the root of one bottom tree of
        $2^{16}$ slots
  \item one-time signature: WOTS$^+$C~\cite{HK22C}, chains of length 4, and a 128-bit digest read as 64
        base-4 digits. Grinding forces the last 6 digits to 0, so those chains are dropped (58 chains
        left), and the others to sum to $T=106$: $2^{20}$ tries. A forger pays the same grinding, so nothing is lost
  \item every digest is $\hash(P,\mathit{tw},R,M)$, where the signer grinds the 16-byte randomizer $R$
        itself, and $M$ is the attestation (bottom layer) or the bottom root (top layer)
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}
  \item signature: 2 halves of 1\,200 bytes each. The top half is the same for $2^{16}$ slots, so it can
        be sent once and cached
  \item verification: $101+100=201$ compressions
  \item keygen: $16\cdot10^6$ compressions for the top tree, and the same for each bottom tree
\end{itemize}

\section{Threshold signing}

\begin{itemize}
  \item $t$-of-$n$ with $n=2f+1$ and $t=f+1$ (e.g.\ 2-of-3, 3-of-5, 4-of-7)
  \item malicious security against $t-1$ operators (with abort), at signing and at the ceremonies
  \item no trusted dealer: a DKG ceremony among the $n$ operators at keygen, then one per bottom tree
        (every $2^{16}$ slots, about 3 days with 4-second slots). A dealer would have to come back every 3
        days, so only the DKG is considered
  \item signing: no MPC, 976 bytes per signer, 3 rounds
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Idea (adapted from PRAWNS~\cite{PRAWNS}).} Let $F$ be a threshold PRF: any $t$ operators evaluate
it together, $t-1$ learn nothing. A chain of a bottom key starts at $y_0=F(x_0)$, with $y_{j+1}=\hash(y_j)$.
The ceremony computes the chains in MPC and publishes $y_1\oplus F(x_1)$, $y_2\oplus F(x_2)$ and $y_3$, for
distinct addresses $x_j$. To sign position $p$, the signers reveal $F(x_p)$, which is $y_0$ or unmasks $y_1$
or $y_2$ (nothing for $p=3$).

\paragraph{$F$.} As in leanSPHINCS: one seed $k_T$ per set $T$ of $t-1$ operators, held by the others, and
$F(x)=\bigoplus_T\hash(k_T,x)$. When a quorum $J$ signs $m$, each signer's part of $F(x)$ carries masks
$\hash(k_{ij},x,m,J)$, one per co-signer $j$, which cancel within the signing: parts from different
signings don't combine.

\paragraph{Ceremony.} Honest-majority MPC with replicated sharing~\cite{CDI05} and a malicious-security
check~\cite{GS20}, with abort:
\begin{itemize}
  \item at keygen: generate $P$, the seeds and the pairwise keys $k_{ij}$ (commit-reveal); MPC hashes the
        chains of the $2^{16}$ top keys and opens their ends; the top root is the public key. Top keys
        only sign bottom roots, known at the ceremonies, so they need no masks
  \item for each bottom tree: MPC hashes the chains of its $2^{16}$ keys ($58\times3$ hashes per key) and
        opens their ends and masked values; the tree is built in the clear; MPC recomputes the next top
        key and opens its signature of the bottom root
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Signing (any $t$, no MPC).}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item agree on (slot, $m$)
  \item each signer commits to a random $r_i$, then reveals it: $R_0=\hash(r_1,\dots,r_t,m)$; grind
        $R=\hash(R_0,\mathit{ctr})$ for the WOTS$^+$C encoding ($2^{20}$ tries, in the clear)
  \item each signer sends its parts of $F$ at the selected positions; combine, unmask, and check each value
        against its chain end
\end{enumerate}
The slashing-protection state records the $R$ of each slot, so a slot never opens two encodings.

\paragraph{Ceremony cost (measured with the leanSPHINCS prototype).} $2^{16}\cdot58\cdot3=11.4\cdot10^6$ MPC
hashes per bottom tree, and once at keygen. Per operator:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
  & traffic & average rate (4-second slots) \\
  2-of-3 & 19.2~GB & 73~KB/s \\
  3-of-5 & 23.1~GB & 88~KB/s \\
  4-of-7 & 24.8~GB & 94~KB/s \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent Public data: 182~MB per bottom tree (masked values and chain ends). Operators keep only their
seeds and pairwise keys.
